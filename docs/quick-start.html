DepthSight — Quick Start

From signup to your first strategy in 5 minutes

Seven steps. No code. No spreadsheets. Test on paper money first, then go live when you're ready.

1

Sign in (30 sec)

Go to https://depthsight.diverseinc.net/login. Use the email/password from your welcome email, or click Continue with Google.

2

Open the Hub (10 sec)

Click Hub in the left sidebar. You'll see 7 verified strategy templates — each is a complete, working strategy you can use as-is.

3

Pick one and import (30 sec)

Click Use Template on any strategy. RSI Breakout v2 is the most popular starting point. The strategy loads in the editor with all the configuration done for you.

4

Try the AI Co-pilot (1 min)

In the editor, find the AI Co-pilot panel. Type a strategy idea in plain English and click Generate. You'll get a complete strategy config in 3-8 seconds. Apply it to your editor.

5

Run on paper trading (instant)

At the top of the editor, set mode = Paper (no real money). Click Start. The strategy runs against live market data with $10,000 fake USDT. Watch it in the dashboard.

6

Connect exchange (when ready, 2 min)

Settings → API Keys → Add Exchange Key → Binance or Bybit. Create a key on the exchange with read + trade permission (NOT withdraw), paste it here. The system validates it in seconds.

7

Switch to live (when confident)

In the editor, switch from Paper to Live. Click Start. Real trades on the real exchange. Start small (2-5% position size) and scale up as the strategy proves itself.

That’s it. You’re trading.
